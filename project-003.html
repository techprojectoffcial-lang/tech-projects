Humanoid Robots

Tech Projects · Running Projects
🤖

Humanoid robots are being developed to perform tasks in environments designed for people, including factories, warehouses, laboratories and potentially homes.

What they need

A useful humanoid robot needs perception, balance, manipulation, navigation and software that can make decisions in changing environments.

Why companies are building them

Human-shaped machines could potentially use existing workspaces and tools without requiring every environment to be redesigned for automation.

Current status

Humanoid robotics is an active field with many prototypes and commercial pilots. Capability, safety, cost and reliability are still major development challenges.
