Self-Healing Concrete

Tech Projects · Tested & Developing
🏗️

Self-healing concrete is designed to reduce the impact of small cracks by using embedded healing mechanisms. Depending on the research approach, bacteria or other materials can help form new mineral material when cracks allow water to enter.

How it works

A crack can allow moisture into the concrete. In some approaches, dormant bacteria become active and produce minerals that help seal the crack. Other approaches use capsules or chemical healing agents.

Why it matters

If small cracks can be sealed before they grow, structures may require less maintenance and could have a longer service life. Potential applications include buildings, bridges, tunnels and other concrete infrastructure.

Current status

Different self-healing concrete approaches have been tested in laboratory and pilot conditions. It remains an active research and development field rather than a universal replacement for conventional concrete.
